What you get

Free score: a one-page document with a shop score out of 100 and your top 5 fixes, based on the 3–5 listings you send.

Rewrite (£29, one-off): a document with a new title, 13 tags and a description for up to 25 listings, plus a before/after report and a short to-do list for you. We deliver a document only. We don't change anything in your shop; you choose what to copy across.

We deliver within 48 hours of receiving your listings (pasted in an email or as your own Shop Manager CSV).

Where we don't have a detail, such as a size or material, we mark it [CONFIRM] rather than make it up.

No login or access

We never need or ask for your Etsy password, login or shop access. Please don't send them.

No guarantees on ranking or sales

We can't guarantee search ranking, views or sales. We make your listings clearer and more complete, which gives them a better chance.

Revisions and refunds

Your rewrite includes one free revision: tell us what to change and we'll send an updated document.

You get a full refund if we miss the 48-hour delivery time, or if you ask before we've started work.

Your data

Under UK GDPR, we use the details you send only to prepare your score and report. We delete them after 90 days. Contact shoppolish@cagroup.uk for any data question or to ask us to delete your details sooner.

Who it's for

UK-based Etsy sellers only, for now. If you're not UK-based we'll politely decline and delete your details.
