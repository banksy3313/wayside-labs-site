Nourish Weekly support

Email support@waysidelabs.co.uk and include your device and whether you subscribed with Apple or Google. We aim to reply to that address.

Restore purchases

Open the Profile screen, tap “View membership”, then “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that you used to subscribe. The app confirms “Purchases restored”, or it says that no active membership was found. If you paid and restore still finds nothing, email support with the store receipt.

Cancel a subscription

Cancel in the store. Deleting the app does not stop the renewal.

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, then Nourish Weekly. You can also use the App Store subscriptions page.
  • Android: Google Play Store, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, then Nourish Weekly.

Cancel at least 24 hours before a trial or paid period ends to avoid the next charge. Refunds are requested from Apple or Google, not from inside the app.

Delete your data

There is no account. To clear what the app has saved, either:

  • on the Profile screen, tap “Reset test data” and confirm Reset, which replaces your saved details with the sample profile; or
  • uninstall Nourish Weekly.

On Android, a Google account backup can keep a copy after uninstall. Turn off or delete that app backup in Android’s backup settings if you want it gone. On iPhone and iPad, the app’s saved data is not included in iCloud backup.

Reset and uninstall do not cancel Premium and do not delete the store’s payment record. For deletion of purchase data held for the app, email support@waysidelabs.co.uk with the store you used and any transaction reference from the receipt.

Units

On the Profile screen, under About you, choose “kg & cm” or “lb & in”. The Profile weight and height fields follow that choice. Weight check-ins on the Trends screen are entered and stored in kilograms, including when the profile is set to pounds. Enter the Trends figure in kilograms.
