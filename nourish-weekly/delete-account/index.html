Delete your Nourish Weekly account and data

App name: Nourish Weekly. Developer: Wayside Labs.

Delete your account in the app

In the app, open Profile, then Settings, then “Delete account”, and confirm. On the paywall, open Account, then “Delete account”.

Delete your account without the app

Email support@waysidelabs.co.uk from the account’s email address. Use the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

We delete the account (the email address), and the saved profile, plans and app data held on our servers. That deletes the Supabase account: the signed-in user, the email address, the account ID, and the records kept so the subscription emails can be sent. The app then signs you out.

What may be kept

The plan and profile on the phone stay until you uninstall the app. Deleting the account does not clear that on-device copy. If the phone’s own backup is on, that backup can keep a copy until you delete it in the phone’s backup settings. Wayside Labs cannot read that backup. This app does not switch phone backup on or off.

A food diary you share is then kept by the service you sent it to, under that service’s policy. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep the subscription record under their own policies.

Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription, and it does not delete the store’s payment record. The app does not itself delete the RevenueCat record of the account ID and email. To ask us to delete purchase data we hold through RevenueCat, email support@waysidelabs.co.uk. You can also use the support page. Tell us whether you subscribed with Apple or Google and, if you have it, the transaction reference from the store receipt. Requests about Apple’s or Google’s own payment records go through the store.

Cancelling a subscription

Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription in the App Store or Google Play. Cancel in the store. The steps are on the support page:

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, then Nourish Weekly. You can also use the App Store subscriptions page.
  • Android: Google Play Store, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, then Nourish Weekly.

Cancel at least 24 hours before a trial or paid period ends to avoid the next charge. Refunds are requested from Apple or Google, not from inside the app.
