Privacy policy

Nourish Weekly is published by Carl Banks, trading as Wayside Labs, of Wayside House, Ipsley, B98 0AL, United Kingdom. This policy explains what the app does with personal data. It was last updated on 24 November 2026.

Questions and privacy requests go to support@waysidelabs.co.uk.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Carl Banks, trading as Wayside Labs, Wayside House, Ipsley, B98 0AL, United Kingdom.

Registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): registration number ZC269049

Who the app is for

Nourish Weekly is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not want data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will help you remove what we can.

Your account

You need an account to use Nourish Weekly. You sign in with your email address. We send a 6-digit code to that address, from hello@waysidelabs.co.uk, and you enter the code in the app.

Supabase hosts the account. It stores your email address and an account ID. The app also keeps the sign-in session on the device so you stay signed in.

We use the account to let you in, to match your subscription to you, and to send the service emails described below. The lawful basis is contract. This processing is part of providing the subscription you asked for.

Data stored on your device

The details you enter, and the plans the app builds from them, are saved on the phone or tablet in the app’s own storage. That record is used to calculate calorie targets, build the two-week meal plan, keep your shopping list and show your progress. It is not uploaded with your account.

Your details (such as age, height, weight and goal) are stored on your phone until you create an account. You can clear them from the first-day screen.

That on-device record can include:

  • a profile name, which is optional, plus body weight, height, age and a sex value used in the calorie estimate;
  • your choice of units (kilograms, pounds, or stones and pounds), and whether the takeaway list is the UK or US list;
  • goal and pace, daily movement, and exercise sessions, type, intensity and length;
  • how many meals and snacks you want each day, and foods and flavours you mark as liked or avoided;
  • optional trainer-set calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat;
  • the generated weeks, meals you add, remove, replace or save as favourites, including takeaway meals from the built-in list, and custom meal names and macros you type;
  • weight check-ins, saved in kilograms with the date, a short history of daily calorie and macro totals, and which shopping items you have ticked.

The app reads the device region on the phone to suggest the UK or US takeaway list. You can change that choice on the Profile screen. Body measurements, the meal plan and that region choice stay on the device. They are not stored in the Supabase account.

Until you finish setup, the app can show a sample profile, including a name, a male sex value and sample body measurements. Setup replaces it. You can edit the name, sex, weight, height, age and the other fields above on the Profile screen. Setup does not continue if the age is under 18.

The app does not ask for a medicine name, dose, prescriber or clinical record.

When data leaves the device

Sign-in

When you ask for a code, the app sends your email address to Supabase. Supabase Auth sends the 6-digit code through Resend, our email processor, from hello@waysidelabs.co.uk.

Subscriptions

The Apple App Store or Google Play handles the subscription and any free trial, and takes the payment. Wayside Labs does not receive your card or bank details. Apple and Google keep their own record of the transaction under their policies.

The app uses RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether the subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your account ID and the email address you signed in with. The app does not send RevenueCat your name, weight, age, meals or diary.

Before you sign in, the app sets a few anonymous timestamps on the anonymous RevenueCat customer ID, to measure the sign-up steps: onboarding started, plan viewed, paywall viewed and sign-in completed. These contain no profile or meal plan data.

When the subscription changes, RevenueCat notifies our service. To time the emails below, we store the account ID, the email address, the store transaction reference, the product, the price and currency, and the relevant dates.

Service emails about the subscription

We email the address on your account about the subscription. These are service messages about the subscription, not marketing. They are separate from the email collected on the free meal plan page. Resend sends them. The lawful basis is contract. The messages are:

  • a reminder 3 days before a free trial ends;
  • on a yearly plan, a reminder 30 days before renewal, and again 7 days before;
  • on a monthly plan, a reminder 7 days before the renewal at each 6-month point;
  • a note on the day a 14-day cancellation window starts;
  • a note when the subscription has ended.

A message can name the date the period ends and, when we have it, the store price and currency. It does not include your weight, meals or profile. If the account has been deleted, the message is not sent.

Food diary you choose to send

If you tap “Share food diary”, the app opens your device’s share sheet with a plain-text summary of the two planned weeks (calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat for each day) and your current weight in the units on your profile. It goes only to the app or person you pick. Wayside Labs does not receive a copy.

If you tap a source link on a meal, the device opens that page. The app does not add your name or account to the address.

Who else receives data

  • Supabase, which hosts the account and the records used to send the subscription emails. Supabase is our processor for that.
  • Resend, which sends the sign-in code and the subscription service emails. Resend is our email processor.
  • RevenueCat, Inc., which checks whether the subscription is active. Before sign-in we set the anonymous timestamps described above on the anonymous RevenueCat customer ID. After sign-in we give RevenueCat the account ID and the sign-in email.
  • Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), which take payment and restore purchases.
  • Whatever app you choose in the share sheet, and only if you send the diary.

Meal photographs are included in the app. They are not loaded from a photo website.

We do not sell personal data. The app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

How long it is kept

We keep your email address, account ID and the records used to send the subscription emails until you delete the account in the app. Deleting the account deletes them. We have not set a further retention period. If you believe we still hold a copy, email support@waysidelabs.co.uk and ask us to delete it. We will delete what we still hold.

The plan and profile on the phone stay until you uninstall the app. Deleting the account does not clear that on-device copy. If the phone’s own backup is on, that backup can keep a copy until you delete it in the phone’s backup settings. Wayside Labs cannot read that backup. This app does not switch phone backup on or off.

A food diary you share is then kept by the service you sent it to, under that service’s policy. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep the subscription record under their own policies.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Profile, then Settings, then “Delete account”, and confirm. On the paywall, open Account, then “Delete account”. That deletes the Supabase account: the signed-in user, the email address, the account ID, and the records kept so the subscription emails can be sent. The app then signs you out.

Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription, and it does not delete the store’s payment record. Cancel the subscription in the store. The plan saved on the phone also stays until you uninstall Nourish Weekly. If a phone backup of the app remains, delete that backup as well if you want it gone.

The app does not itself delete the RevenueCat record of the account ID and email. To ask us to delete purchase data we hold through RevenueCat, email support@waysidelabs.co.uk. You can also use the support page. Tell us whether you subscribed with Apple or Google and, if you have it, the transaction reference from the store receipt. Requests about Apple’s or Google’s own payment records go through the store.

The steps above, and how to ask if you no longer have the app, are on the delete your account and data page.

Your rights

If UK data protection law applies, you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and you can ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict it, or send you a portable copy. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

The practical way to delete the account is in the app, as described above. The practical way to remove the plan is to uninstall the app. Email support@waysidelabs.co.uk if you want help, or if the request is about a purchase.

We use the on-device data to provide the plan you asked for. We use the account, the sign-in code and the service emails to provide the subscription. We use the subscription record to check that membership is active and to restore it on a later install. The lawful basis for the account and the service emails is contract.

Email sign-up

This section covers only the email sign-up on the Wayside Labs website, not the app. The Nourish Weekly page (/nourish-weekly/) does not collect an email address. The free meal plan page (/nourish-weekly/free-meal-plan/) collects an email address so we can send the free meal plan.

After you submit the form, the page shows a link to download the free meal-plan PDF. The PDF itself collects nothing.

We collect your email address, an optional first name, and the consent you gave.

We also record the version of the consent wording you agreed to, the date and time you signed up, and the page address (including any query string). When the link you opened includes them, we collect utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign, and a source value taken from utm_source or, if that is absent, from the referring page.

We use these details to send the free meal plan and to see which link the sign-up came from. The form does not load analytics or other third-party scripts. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Sign-ups are stored in Airtable, sent there through Make.com. Both act as our data processors, and your details may be processed outside the UK under their standard data protection safeguards.

We keep your details for 12 months, or until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, whichever is sooner.

To unsubscribe, or to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of those details, email support@waysidelabs.co.uk. You can also reply to any Nourish Weekly email and ask to be removed.

Transfers outside the UK

Supabase, Resend, RevenueCat, Apple and Google may process the data described above outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where the law requires a safeguard for that transfer, the safeguard is the provider’s contractual terms or another lawful transfer mechanism.
