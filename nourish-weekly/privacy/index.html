Privacy policy

Nourish Weekly is published by Carl Banks, trading as Wayside Labs, of Wayside House, Ipsley, B98 0AL, United Kingdom. This policy explains what the app does with personal data. It was last updated on 6 October 2026.

Questions and privacy requests go to support@waysidelabs.co.uk.

Who the app is for

Nourish Weekly is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not want data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will help you remove what we can.

There is no account

The app has no sign-in and no account. It does not ask for your email address, and it does not send a name or email address to us.

Data stored on your device

The details you enter, and the plans the app builds from them, are saved on the phone or tablet in the app’s own storage. Wayside Labs does not run a server that receives this information. It is used to calculate calorie targets, build the two-week meal plan, keep your shopping list and show your progress.

That on-device record can include:

  • a profile name, body weight, height, age and a sex value used in the calorie estimate;
  • your choice of units (kilograms and centimetres, or pounds and inches);
  • goal and pace, daily movement, and exercise sessions, type, intensity and length;
  • how many meals and snacks you want each day, and foods and flavours you mark as liked;
  • whether you chose the everyday plan or the “Coming off GLP-1” planning route, and which stage you selected;
  • optional trainer-set calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat;
  • the generated weeks, meals you add, remove, replace or favourite, custom meal names and macros you type, and restaurant meals you add from the built-in list;
  • weight check-ins with the date, a short history of daily calorie and macro totals, and which shopping items you have ticked.

A new install starts from a sample profile so the plan can be shown immediately. The sample includes a name, a male sex value and sample body measurements. The current version has no control to edit the name or the sex value, and the sex value is still used when the app estimates calories. Weight, height, age and the other fields above can be edited on the Profile screen.

The “Coming off GLP-1” choice is a planning setting. The app does not ask for a medicine name, dose, prescriber or clinical record.

When data leaves the device

Three things can leave the device. Nothing else in the app sends your profile, weight, meals or diary to Wayside Labs.

Subscriptions

Membership is handled by RevenueCat, Inc. When the app checks whether a subscription is active, or you buy or restore one, RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier that it creates for that install, the store product, the purchase or restore result, and technical details of the request such as device type, operating system, locale, currency and IP address. The app does not give RevenueCat your name, email, weight, age or meal plan, and it does not turn on advertising identifiers.

Apple or Google processes the payment. Wayside Labs does not receive your card or bank details. Apple and Google also keep their own record of the transaction under their policies.

Meal photographs

Meal pictures are loaded from Unsplash and Pexels when a meal is shown. Those services receive the ordinary details of an image request, including your IP address and the address of the photograph. The app does not attach your name or profile to that request.

Food diary you choose to send

If you tap “Download or send food diary”, the app opens your device’s share sheet with a plain-text summary of the two planned weeks (calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat for each day) and your current weight in kilograms. It goes only to the app or person you pick. Wayside Labs does not receive a copy.

Who else receives data

  • RevenueCat, Inc., to validate the subscription and tell the app whether Premium is active.
  • Apple (App Store and StoreKit) or Google (Google Play Billing), to take payment and restore purchases.
  • Unsplash and Pexels, to deliver meal photographs.
  • On Android, Google, if the device’s backup is switched on. See “Backups” below.
  • Whatever app you choose in the share sheet, and only if you send the diary.

We do not sell personal data. The app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Backups

On iPhone and iPad, the app’s saved data is kept out of iCloud backup. Deleting the app removes that copy.

On Android, backup of app data is allowed. If Android backup is on, Google may keep a copy in the Google account backup for that device. Wayside Labs cannot read that backup. Uninstalling the app removes the copy on the phone, and the Google account backup can remain until you delete it in Android’s backup settings.

How long it is kept

The on-device record stays until you reset it in the app or uninstall the app, and, on Android, until a device backup of it is deleted. A food diary you share is then kept by the service you sent it to, under that service’s policy. Purchase records stay with RevenueCat until you ask us to delete them, and with Apple or Google for as long as those companies keep store transactions.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. You can remove the data in either of these ways:

  • On the Profile screen, tap “Reset test data” and confirm. This replaces the saved profile, plans, favourites, weight check-ins and shopping ticks with the sample profile.
  • Uninstall Nourish Weekly. On Android, also delete the app’s backup in your Google account if you do not want a restore to bring the data back.

Neither of those steps cancels a subscription or deletes the store’s record of a payment. To ask us to delete purchase data we hold through RevenueCat, email support@waysidelabs.co.uk. Tell us whether you subscribed with Apple or Google and, if you have it, the transaction reference from the store receipt. Apple and Google keep their own payment records, and requests about those records go through the store.

Your rights

If UK data protection law applies, you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and you can ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict it, or send you a portable copy. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. Because the profile and plans stay on your device, the practical way to see, correct or delete them is in the app, or by uninstalling it. Email us if you want help, or if the request is about a purchase.

We use the on-device data to provide the plan you asked for. We use purchase data to provide membership, to prevent fraudulent purchases, and to restore a subscription on a later install.

Transfers outside the UK

RevenueCat, Apple, Google, Unsplash and Pexels may process the data described above outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where the law requires a safeguard for that transfer, the safeguard is the provider’s contractual terms or another lawful transfer mechanism.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
