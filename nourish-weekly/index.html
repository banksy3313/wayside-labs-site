Nourish Weekly

Nourish Weekly is a meal-planning app from Wayside Labs. You enter your weight, height, age and routine, and it builds a two-week plan, a shopping list and a simple food diary from recipes included with the app. Those details stay on your device. It is for adults aged 18 and over, and the nutrition content is planning information, not medical advice.
