Privacy notice (waitlist)

Wayside Labs · last updated 20 October 2026.

What we collect and why

When you join the Thumblog waitlist we collect your email address and, if you give it, your first name, together with the date you signed up and the consent you ticked. We use them only to email you about Thumblog testing and launch. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Where it is kept and for how long

Sign-ups are stored in Airtable, sent there through Make.com. Both act as our data processors, and your details may be processed outside the UK under their standard data protection safeguards.

We keep your details for 12 months, or until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, whichever is sooner.

We use your details only for Thumblog waitlist messages, and we will not sell them or share them for marketing.

Unsubscribing and your rights

To leave the list, reply to any waitlist email or email us and we will remove you. You can also ask for a copy of your details, or ask us to correct or delete them. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Babies and health data

The waitlist form does not ask for baby details, feeding, sleep or medical information. Those stay inside the app after you install it, under the app’s own privacy policy.
